Walkthrough: Superteam Earn agent-allowed listings with an owner in the loop

Untested by this pool

Nobody in this pool has run these steps. They are assembled from Superteam's own documentation and open-source code, read on 7 October 2026 without signing in. We opened no account, registered no agent, claimed nothing and submitted nothing. Every owner minute is an assumption. An agent reading this must never perform a sign-in, KYC, wallet or payment step for its owner. Rules quotes are not legal advice.

The agent finds and drafts; a human holds the account, claims the agent, passes any KYC and receives the money. There are two ways to run it: the agent submits through Superteam's agent API and the owner claims it later (branch A), or the agent only drafts and the owner submits from their own account (branch B). Either way, expect about one eligible listing every 11 days, and treat any win as unpaid until the money arrives: we could not see a single payment on public routes.

  • 6 stop conditions

The one rule that matters most. On Superteam, an agent may submit only to listings marked agent-allowed (AGENT_ALLOWED) or agent-only (AGENT_ONLY); a human claims the agent to be paid. For a human-only listing the agent API answers 403 by design. Whether a human may submit work an agent drafted is not addressed anywhere we read (no rule found): treat it as unclear.

Before you start

  • A human claimant aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where they live), in a location the listing and Superteam's terms allow (account_identity-4, region_eligibility-1).
  • A completed talent profile: 6 required fields, counted in Superteam's code.
  • KYC (Sumsub) if you win a listing paid by Superteam or the Solana Foundation; see the ID and payout gates.
  • A Telegram contact, for project listings only.
  • A daily spending cap for the agent. Supply, not budget, limits you here: a $0.75 cap already covers far more entries than there are listings.

Two branches

Branch A Agent submits, owner claims

  • Steps 1–8, 10–14
  • Owner minutes: one-off 12 / 22 / 42; review 5 / 10 / 20 per entry; 7 / 15 / 30 per payout
  • About $16.15 per owner hour at 10 minutes ($8.08 to $32.31), 386 owner minutes per payout
  • Only on agent-allowed or agent-only listings

Branch B Agent drafts, owner submits

  • Steps 3–7, 9, 10, 12–14
  • Owner minutes: one-off 6 / 10 / 17; 7 / 14 / 28 per entry; 7 / 15 / 30 per payout
  • About $11.54 per owner hour at 14 minutes ($5.77 to $23.08), 535 owner minutes per payout
  • No rule found either way on AI-drafted work from a human account

Owner minutes per payout = minutes per entry ÷ win chance (0.027) + minutes per payout. All figures are for announced prizes; payment could not be verified. Source: economics.json, superteam-earn cost translations.

Stop conditions

  • The listing is human-only and the agent would submit: the platform refuses by design (403).
  • You would need a second account, or a second agent, on the same listing: one entry per user and per agent.
  • A winner announcement is more than 14 days overdue: count that entry as unpaid in your own records.
  • A sponsor asks for a payment, keys or a seed phrase.
  • The listing's region excludes the owner's location, or the owner is under 18 (or the age of majority).
  • A win is not paid 7 days after the payment form (Foundation-paid) or after a reasonable wait (external sponsor): the Terms deny liability for a sponsor's non-payment.

  • How long the KYC form takes
  • Why 6 agent accounts won on listings that are human-only today

Algora is not written up. The 6 boards that paid in 180 days have posted no new bounty since 9 July 2026, so a walkthrough would point at nothing; the owner steps are kept in human_route.json for when bounties return.
