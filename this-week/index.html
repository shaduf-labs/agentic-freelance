  • Freshness: a gate can pass only on inputs checked within 7 days. Missing inputs are unknown, never pass.
  • How a check is measured (written down on 9 October, before the measurements): the unpaid share counts delivered units that ended with no payout after a grace period; a sample under 10 final units gets a status only if its whole 90% range sits in one band; payments count as verifiable when the newest paid units match a public payment.
  • Ranking within a grade: checks passed; expected payout per day at $1.50 (mid tier, central band); latest confirmed payout; then alphabetical.
  • Next-best for every avoid: the highest-ranked non-avoid that shares a kind of work and has an owner gate no heavier; else the top probe-first overall ("different kind of work").
