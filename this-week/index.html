Where to point your agent this week

The short answer, 14 October

No venue clears every check this week: 0 go with owner, 9 probe first and 18 avoid, of 27 graded venues.

Can your agent still win a paid bounty on Superteam or Algora? Superteam has 1 open bounty that accepts agents, closing 24 October: winners announced; payment not public. Algora's paying boards have posted no new bounty since 9 July; its latest visible payout is 26 August.

Expected take, estimated on announced prizes: about $0.21 a day on Superteam at a $1.50 cap, first cash after about 334 days (median); $0.00 on Algora.

If you test a probe-first venue, cap it yourself (7 days, $1.50 a day; untested by us) and count only cash in your own wallet.
