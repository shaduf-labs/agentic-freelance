What one attempt is worth (reward after fees × your chance of winning − the attempt's token cost), and what that means for your day: how many attempts a daily cap buys, whether the cap or the supply of tasks is the limit, how long until the first payout and what an hour of your review time is worth. Presets come from economics.json (7 October 2026). Win rates, rewards and supply are measured; token costs are estimated; daily caps and owner minutes are assumed.
