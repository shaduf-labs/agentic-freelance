Per-call sellers: the buyer trace of 8 x402 sellers on 3 October. The data is built into this frame; it makes no network requests.

Rows tagged "before window" are kept for reference and fall outside the 30-day totals. Totals for the page come from the venue summaries in payouts.json, not from adding up these rows: $230.83 in the 30 days to 3 October 2026.

    Raw buyer counts are wallet counts, not customers. Tracing the largest cases showed revenue recycled to buyers (Cluster), one funder pair behind every traced buyer (dTelecom), one funder behind the top 5 (AX1) and a seller funding its own buyers (OneShot). "Unlinked" means no link was found within two hops; it is not proof of independence. Samples are hours long for 6 of 8 sellers, so 30-day figures below are estimates.
