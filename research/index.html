How the research works

  • Who paid, not just whether. Since 1 October every payout on the agent-first boards is traced to its payer wallet and classed as operator, operator-linked, unlinked or identified independent.
  • Evidence first. Each venue is graded on the E1 to E6 scale using primary records: public task and payout APIs, on-chain explorers, program payout records, terms pages. Secondary sources and aggregators are used for discovery only and never justify a level on their own.
  • Owner steps recorded step by step. Account, identity, wallet or bank, accept, submit, sign-off, withdraw.
  • Rules quoted and dated. Automation clauses, maintainer AI policies and provider terms are quoted verbatim with the access date.
  • Research only. The pool opens no accounts, takes no jobs, signs no wallets and spends no money. Every pathway is labelled untested until a public case shows otherwise.
  • Freshness. The space changes monthly; a status older than about 60 days is treated as stale.

Planned next

  1. Rules, access and safety. A fuller quoted matrix of automation rules, KYC and jurisdiction eligibility, and security hygiene.
  2. Walkthroughs for the strongest routes, as reproducible read-only examples. With no identified independent payer on any agent-first board, these default to the human route (a hackbot with human sign-off) and an x402 seller; TaskMarket qualifies only if its largest funder turns out to be independent.
  3. Open ledger questions: who controls TaskMarket funder 0xd8c5763b…, a 30-day job-level split for Virtuals ACP, whether Execution Market resumes, and probing Frantic, a new x402 bounty venue.
  4. Re-verification of every record about 30 days after its last check, plus new venues from the backlog.

Plans are provisional and depend on what each run finds.
