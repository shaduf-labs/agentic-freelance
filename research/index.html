How the research works

  • Who paid, not just whether. Since 1 October every payout on the agent-first boards is traced to its payer wallet and classed as operator, operator-linked, unlinked or identified independent. Since 3 October the same is done for samples of x402 buyers; raw buyer counts are never read as customers.
  • Evidence first. Each venue is graded on the E1 to E6 scale using primary records: public task and payout APIs, on-chain explorers, program payout records, terms pages. Secondary sources and aggregators are used for discovery only and never justify a level on their own.
  • Owner steps recorded step by step. Account, identity, wallet or bank, accept, submit, sign-off, withdraw.
  • Communities measured, not described. Since 4 October each agent community gets a dated status from its own newest items (items per day, distinct authors, top-author share), a read-only scan of those items, and a "should my agent join?" verdict from a fixed rule. Join steps are recorded from each site’s documents and stay untested.
  • Rules quoted and dated. Since 2 October every automation, account, KYC, maintainer and provider clause is a row in a rules matrix: quoted verbatim, given a verdict (forbid, restrict, allow, unclear), dated, and labelled by how it was read (primary, archive copy, platform JSON, or unverified snippet). Verdicts describe the text, not enforcement; none of it is legal advice.
  • Security from incidents, not hunches. Owner safety controls are tied to documented incidents with a primary source, plus a read-only scan of public task text.
  • Research only. The pool opens no accounts, takes no jobs, signs no wallets, builds no payment headers and spends no money. Every pathway and walkthrough is labelled untested until a public case shows otherwise.
  • Corrections are dated, not silent. When a finding is overturned, the dated report keeps its original text with a visible correction note, and the change is logged.
  • Freshness. The space changes monthly; a status older than about 60 days is treated as stale.
