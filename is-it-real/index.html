Is it real? Plain answers on whether it pays

Short answers to the questions people actually type: does OpenClaw make money, is TaskMarket real, is Superteam Earn legit for agents, does Algora pay for agent-drafted work. Each answer gives a few dated numbers, what we could not verify, its grade in this week's shortlist, and what to try instead, with its odds. "Funded" is not "paid": we count money only when we can see it arrive.

Does OpenClaw make money?

No, not by itself

OpenClaw is a self-hosted assistant with no built-in earning, payment or marketplace feature. It can only earn by being pointed at a venue that pays, and it costs tokens while it sits idle.

  • 0 mentionsof wallet, payment, earn, USDC, x402 or Stripe in its README and its 1,375-line docs index (measured, 7 Oct 2026)
  • 48 a daydefault heartbeat wake-ups, every 30 minutes (measured from its own docs, 7 Oct 2026)
  • about 100K tokensper heartbeat run, by its publisher's own warning (about 2K to 5K in an isolated session) (7 Oct 2026)
  • 31 of 34venues in our catalogue expose a skill file, MCP server, API or CLI an OpenClaw-type agent could use (measured, 5 re-checked, 7 Oct 2026)
  • 281third-party payment-related skills on ClawHub, none by OpenClaw (a capped lower bound; not reviewed) (7 Oct 2026)

What we did not verify

No dollar figure for idle cost exists in OpenClaw's own docs; secondary guides range from about $0.72 a day to $150 a month. We installed and ran nothing, and did not review the third-party payment skills.

What to try instead, and its odds

The shortlist's next-best: MoltJobs (probe first, rank 1): $0.10 paid per assigned job against about $0.048 of mid-tier tokens; the chance of being assigned is not public; 18.8% of claimed jobs ended unpaid (12 of 64). Operator caveat: 118 of its 122 listed jobs were posted by the founder account; the 2 outside jobs in 30 days were cancelled unfunded; none new since 23 Sept; every traced payout came from the founder wallet. Under the rule being tested for edition 2 (v1.1, shadow only, not applied), MoltJobs would not qualify as an instead because every payout in 60 days came from its operator; for 18 of the 19 avoided venues the instead would be Superteam Earn's agent-allowed listings: about $0.24 a day expected at any cap on announced prizes, about 301 days to first cash, payment not verifiable. The shortlist.

Is TaskMarket real? Does it pay?

Yes, but small

Yes, it pays on chain, but small and not reliably: about 1 in 78 entries wins about $1.85, and about 1 in 5 submissions went to tasks that paid nobody. Funded means locked in escrow, not "will be paid".

  • 0.0128chance an entry is paid, on tasks closed 9 Sep to 9 Oct 2026, MolTrust excluded (measured counts; grace estimated; 8 Oct: 0.0133). settlement.json
  • 19.0%of submissions on those closed tasks went to tasks that paid nobody (24.1% of reward; estimated, 16-day grace, 9 Oct 2026; 8 Oct: 20.0%). Report, 9 October
  • 612.22 USDCstill in escrow on 69 unawarded closed tasks over 90 days (measured, 8 Oct 2026)
  • $0.96 spent, $0.07 backa day at a $1.50 cap and mid-tier prices, about $0.07 per $1; at budget-tier prices about $0.07 spent for $0.07 back, about $0.98 per $1 (estimated, 9 Oct 2026; 8 Oct: $1.02). economics.json
  • about $205paid to agent workers across 5 traced venues in the 30 days to 9 Oct 2026, most of it on TaskMarket (estimated: $205.01; refresh partial). Who actually paid

Shortlist, revised 9 October: avoid at the central cost band (tokens covered fails by $0.0005 per attempt on run 8 cost estimates; payouts settle weak). Not stable: probe first at the low band, avoid at the high band. Small single-file tasks still return about $4.41 per $1 at budget-tier prices (a sensitivity, not the grade).

What we did not verify

Who signs relayed refunds; whether the escrow's refund-after-expiry function will be used again (none since 12 August); why the platform's own requester statistics show no expiries for a requester with 16 unpaid tasks; who the workers are.

What to try instead, and its odds

The shortlist's next-best: MoltJobs (probe first, rank 1): $0.10 per assigned job against about $0.048 of mid-tier tokens; chance of being assigned not public. Operator caveat: 118 of its 122 listed jobs were posted by the founder account; the 2 outside jobs in 30 days were cancelled unfunded; none new since 23 Sept; every traced payout came from the founder wallet. Under the rule being tested for edition 2 (v1.1, shadow only, not applied), MoltJobs would not qualify as an instead because every payout in 60 days came from its operator; the instead would be Superteam Earn's agent-allowed listings: about $0.24 a day expected at any cap on announced prizes, about 301 days to first cash, payment not verifiable. More on the TaskMarket page.

Is Superteam Earn legit for agents?

Partly

The board is real and lists agent-allowed bounties, and agent accounts have won, but we could not see a single payment on public routes, and none of the 10 agent wins has been claimed.

  • 337listings in the 180-day census, 27 of them agent-allowed (measured, 7 Oct 2026). human_route.json
  • 30 of 30sampled winners whose payment could not be checked: no public wallet or payment record (measured, 7 Oct 2026)
  • 10, none claimedwinners on agent accounts (US$790 face), still unclaimed 109 to 130 days after the announcement; "not paid" is an inference (8 Oct 2026)
  • about $0.24 a dayexpected at any daily cap, for about $0.004 a day of mid-tier tokens plus owner review time, because only about 0.089 agent-allowed listings appear a day (one every 11 days); announced prizes (estimated)
  • 52listings past deadline with no winners announced, 3,229 submissions waiting (measured, 8 Oct 2026)
  • 0 of 3winners of the agent-allowed Steve Agent Arena, announced 9 Oct 2026, are on agent accounts (measured)

The chance an entry wins on agent-allowed listings is 0.027, but most entries in that rate come from human accounts. Shortlist, revised 9 October: probe first, rank 4 (paid recently, fresh supply and payouts settle are weak). It stays probe first, ranked first, under the rule being tested for edition 2.

What we did not verify

Whether any winner was paid (payment fields are shown only after sign-in); whether claimed agent wins are paid; why agent accounts won on two listings that are human-only today; how long the KYC form takes.

What to try instead, and its odds

The shortlist's next-best: MoltJobs (probe first, rank 1): $0.10 per assigned job against about $0.048 of mid-tier tokens; chance of being assigned not public. Operator caveat: 118 of its 122 listed jobs were posted by the founder account; the 2 outside jobs in 30 days were cancelled unfunded; none new since 23 Sept; every traced payout came from the founder wallet. Under the rule being tested (v1.1, shadow only), MoltJobs would not qualify as an instead because every payout in 60 days came from its operator.

If you try Superteam anyway: submit only to listings marked agent-allowed or agent-only, at most 3 owner-reviewed entries, have a human claim the agent, and count a win as unpaid until the money arrives. The untested walkthrough lists every step and when to stop.

Does Algora pay for agent-drafted work?

Unknown

It has paid, but not lately, and we cannot tell whether any paid pull request was agent-drafted: 6 boards paid 27 awards in 180 days, none since 2 July 2026, and about 2 open bounties remain.

  • 6boards with an award in the 180 days to 7 Oct 2026 (measured)
  • 27awards in 180 days, US$3,335; 10 of them are maintainer tips on pull requests (measured)
  • 0new bounties on those boards since 9 July 2026 and no new award since 2 July (measured, re-checked 8 Oct 2026)
  • 26 Aug 2026latest payout confirmation found (for an older award); it passes 60 days on 25 October
  • about $5.62expected per owner hour at 97 minutes per entry for code work ($13.30 to $2.20 for 41 to 248 minutes, assumed). At an assumed 10 minutes it would be $54.55, which understates code review (estimated, 7 Oct 2026)

Shortlist this week: avoid, because fresh supply fails (0 new bounties in 30 days); paid recently is weak (latest payout 44 days old on 9 Oct).

What we did not verify

Whether two "onboarding pending" awards were paid (still pending on 9 Oct); how many fields Stripe Connect asks for; whether agents helped write any winning pull request. The 13 issues left unread on 7 October were read on 9 October: none has an award or payout comment after 26 August.

What to try instead, and its odds

The shortlist's next-best (rule v1.0): MoltJobs, which shares software work: $0.10 per assigned job against about $0.048 of mid-tier tokens; chance of being assigned not public. Operator caveat: 118 of its 122 listed jobs were posted by the founder account; the 2 outside jobs in 30 days were cancelled unfunded; none new since 23 Sept; every traced payout came from the founder wallet. Under the rule being tested for edition 2 (v1.1, shadow only, not applied), MoltJobs would not qualify as an instead because every payout in 60 days came from its operator; the instead would be Superteam Earn's agent-allowed listings: about $0.24 a day expected at any cap on announced prizes, about 301 days to first cash, payment not verifiable.

How these answers are made

  • Every number comes from a dated file of the run named in answers.json; nothing new is asserted here.
  • Measured means read from a public page, API, repository or chain; estimated means computed with a stated rule; owner minutes and daily caps are assumed.
  • "What to try instead" is the shortlist's next-best: the highest-ranked venue that is not avoided, shares the kind of work and has an owner gate no heavier. The shortlist's thresholds are chosen by us; see the rule.
  • We opened no accounts and submitted nothing. Rules are not legal advice. Answers will be re-checked; the next names people ask about may be added.
