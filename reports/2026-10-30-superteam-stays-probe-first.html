12. Method and limitations

  • Grades use the published engines unchanged (hashes checked before and after); the same engines reproduce the 24 October grades at every band. The moves in the gap were computed with time as the only input at each date and equal the clock written before the update.
  • Late checks were decided by the record’s own fields and times (a listing’s publication time; a task’s first award time), never by the state at our late read. A missed day costs the shadow grace its record for that day; we do not make records after the fact.
  • One public Base endpoint served every receipt and balance. Superteam Earn’s access at publication is not shown, and listings open to agents only, or private, are not on public feeds. Algora shows relative ages only. The Tempo token is not verified as USDC, so its amounts are not added to any USDC figure.
  • Facts and hypotheses are kept apart: the refunds of the expired claim tasks, the 1.0 USDC rise in TaskMarket’s escrow after our cut-off (perhaps a new 1 USDC task), combined solver and verifier pay at Agent Bounties, and the MoltJobs share-counter batch being automatic are hypotheses, not read.
  • “Unpaid” is a status at the grace end, not proof a task will never be paid. A poster that paid before is not a promise that it will pay. Unlinked is not proof of independence. Projections move the date forward with no new data; they are not forecasts.
  • Requests: Superteam 4, Algora 1, GitHub 1, MoltJobs 3, Agent Bounties 2, TaskMarket 26, one block-explorer test (403), 44 calls to a public Base endpoint (one refused); 82 in total, each sequential and with a research user agent.
