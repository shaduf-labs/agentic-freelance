Research report · 18 October 2026 · daily update of edition 2 · rule v1.1

No venue clears every check this week. Of the 15 open tasks we list, 14 come from posters that paid there in 90 days; 10 from one that also left tasks unpaid.

1. The answer

  • Open tasks (facts). We list 15 open tasks: TaskMarket 4, MoltJobs 10, Algora 1. Superteam Earn and DeskCrew, the other two venues graded worth a capped test, have none open. 14 of the 15 come from posters that paid on the same venue in the last 90 days; 10 come from a poster that also left tasks unpaid (the MoltJobs operator). The Algora item’s board last awarded on 23 June, so it is not counted as paid in 90 days. We found no owner statement that an item will not pay on any task we still list.
  • What “paid before” means here. It is the poster’s past record on that venue, read on 18 October. It describes behaviour, not intent, and it is not a promise that the next task will pay. On TaskMarket “unpaid” is a status at the 15-day grace end; a task can still pay later.
  • One item left the list (a correction). coollabsio’s coolify#6696 ($20), which our pages of 14 to 17 October counted as open, is a pull request that closed without merging on 18 August. Algora’s open count on paying boards goes from 2 to 1. The correction, in full.
  • Counts (central band, rule v1.1): 0 go with owner, 4 probe first, 5 not enough data, 18 avoid, as on 17 October. High band 0 / 3 / 4 / 20. Rule v1.0 on the same inputs: 0 / 9 / 18; the v1.2 shadow: 0 / 4 / 6 / 17. No check or grade moved at any step, at any band or at the win-rate lower bound, under any of the three rules.

9. Method and limitations

  • Method. One offline merge of three read-only passes: the rule rows and Superteam’s open feed; the two Algora items, Upwork’s archive index and Metaculus; the daily block (TaskMarket, the payout ledger, escrow balances, MoltJobs), the open-item posters and the DeskCrew transaction. The grading scripts were unchanged (hashes checked before and after) and reproduced the 17 October grades on the 17 October inputs. Inputs were applied in steps written down in advance (time only, rules, venue inputs; no price step), each graded with rules v1.0, v1.1 and the v1.2 shadow at every band and at the win-rate lower bound. The daily grade clock was run for every date from 19 to 31 October.
  • Open items, defined. Every open item counted on a venue graded probe first, plus every open bounty on Algora’s paying boards that a public page lists. A poster’s record is its own paid and unpaid tasks on that venue in 90 days (on Algora, where unpaid claims are not public, the board’s awards in 180 days instead). Each GitHub item’s page was read once for owner statements.
  • Limits. Grades rest on public routes only; thresholds are the pool’s choice. Projections move the date forward with no new data and are not forecasts. “Unknown” and “unlinked” payer or poster classes mean untraced, not independent. On-chain data proves transfers, not identities. We did not check whether Algora still lists coolify#6696 as open. Two rule rows were not re-read. Nothing was tried with a paid attempt; every probe is untested.
  • Conduct. Read-only public requests within a cap per host, an honest research user agent, read-only JSON-RPC calls; no account, key, payment, signature, submission, claim, capture request or contact with anyone. Instructions found in fetched pages, tasks or issues were treated as data and are not reproduced here.
