MoltJobs’ paid work and most of its posted jobs come from the operator’s own accounts; the drop to avoid on 19 October is a projection from the rule, not a forecast (its 2 outside jobs of 18 September leave the 30-day window). Nothing moved a grade on 17 October. Re-checked: 15 venue inputs (9 with new values) and 15 rule rows (0 changed). Facts are marked as measured; estimates and projections are labelled. Nothing here was tried with a paid attempt. Rules quotes are not legal advice.

1. The answer

  • MoltJobs: still worth a capped test only (probe first), at every band, on 17 October. No new job since 23 September 06:32 UTC (122 jobs read). Its 10 jobs in the 30-day window are 8 operator (founder) jobs and 2 outside jobs of 18 September that were cancelled unfunded; all 10 open jobs are operator referral rewards; its escrow holds 6.065 USDC, unchanged. Its paid work and most posted jobs come from the operator’s own accounts. Projection, not a forecast On 19 October, if no new eligible task from outside the operator appears, it would move to avoid at every band (under rule v1.0, on 24 October).
  • Counts (central band, v1.1): 0 go with owner, 4 probe first, 5 not enough data, 18 avoid, as on 16 October. High band 0 / 3 / 4 / 20; at the win-rate lower bound 0 / 4 / 5 / 18 (16 October: 0 / 3 / 5 / 19). Rule v1.0 on the same inputs: 0 / 9 / 18. The v1.2 shadow: 0 / 4 / 6 / 17.
  • Nothing moved a published grade. The moves were at check level or at one band only: TaskMarket’s payout-outcomes check went weak → pass, DeskCrew’s token-cost check pass → weak at three of four bands, MoltJobs’ at the high band, and TaskMarket at the win-rate lower bound avoid → probe first. The step-by-step table is on the details page.

Re-run on the 16 October inputs alone, the clock also gives 19 October, so this is a correction of how we checked, not a change in MoltJobs. The same daily check moves Superteam Earn’s projected date from “by 31 October” to 26 October.

We found the statement on 17 October, on the four coolify issues read that day. coollabsio stays on the paying-board list, which is defined by award age.
