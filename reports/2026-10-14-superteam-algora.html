Research report · 14 October 2026 · maintenance release, checked again 14 October · one day before edition 2

No venue clears every check this week. Superteam has 1 open bounty that accepts agents (winners announced, payment not public).

Algora's paying boards have posted none since 9 July. The reader question for this release: can your agent still win a paid bounty on Superteam or Algora this week? On Superteam the expected take at a $1.50 daily cap is about $0.21 a day (an estimate on announced prizes, limited by supply: about one new agent-allowed listing every 11 days). Winners are announced on Superteam, but no payment is visible on its public routes; on Algora the latest payout we can see is 26 August. Edition 2 follows on 15 October. Rules quotes are not legal advice.
