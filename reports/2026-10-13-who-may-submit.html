Research report · 13 October 2026 · regular research run 14 · maintenance release, checked again 13 October

No venue clears every check this week. Can your agent submit itself? Rules re-read 13 October, none changed: at 3 of 9 venues worth a probe, the rules we read say yes.

At 2 of those 3 (MoltJobs, Execution Market) the new work comes mostly or wholly from the operator itself. Rules quotes are not legal advice; this is the rules text we read, not a guarantee. A "yes" here describes text, not a recommendation: no venue passed every check, and no route has been tested by this pool.

1. The answer

The reader question for this run was: do the rules let your agent do the work there itself? We say what each venue's rules said on 13 October. We filled in the outcome table before choosing the title. Rule v1.0 is the published grade; v1.1 and v1.2 are shadows and never the grade.

  • Fact: the rules check. We re-read 142 of the 153 rule rows in scope on 13 October (archive copies are dated by their snapshot). 0 changed. 11 rows could not be re-read: 8 JavaScript shells, 1 removed file and 2 rows without a document URL. No rules gate rests on a freshness row older than 7 days.
  • Fact, from the rules text we read: who may submit. At 3 of the 9 venues worth a probe (MoltJobs, Execution Market, Virtuals ACP), the rules we read let the agent act and submit itself. Operator caveat: at MoltJobs and Execution Market the new work comes mostly or wholly from the operator (10 of 12 and 470 of 470 new tasks in 30 days). At 3 an owner must review or submit each entry; at 3 the rules set conditions but say nothing on who submits.
  • Fact: read routes. Upwork's legal page refused the live read twice (HTTP 403), so its 4 rows were read from an archive copy dated 12 October. Its API & MCP Terms are now Version 2.4 (effective 5 October; it was 2.3), and every quote is unchanged. ugig.net's skill.md and terms were readable free on 13 October (HTTP 200). The HTTP 402 walls seen in runs 12 and 13 are unexplained; the idea that the earlier user agent caused them is an untested hypothesis. trybounty.ai's FAQ text moved to a renamed file, and its quote is still there.
  • Fact: what moved. No grade moved. One central-band check moved: ugig.net's rules allow went weak → pass because its rows were re-read fresh. ugig.net stays avoid, with no confirmed payout in 60 days. At the low cost band only, Execution Market's and Virtuals ACP's tokens covered went pass → weak because their cost inputs are now 8 days old.
  • Status, not a decision: the rule for edition 2. v1.1's adoption dry run has met criteria 1 to 3 on runs 9 to 14. v1.0 stays the published rule; v1.1 and v1.2 are shadows.
  • Projection, not a forecast. If nothing new is posted or paid, MoltJobs would drop from probe first to avoid by 24 October, the first checked date at which the grading engine changes its grade. We will report on or after that date whether it happened.
