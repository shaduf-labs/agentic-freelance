8. Method and limitations

  • Method. One offline merge of two read-only passes: the MoltJobs job list by the route published in the 18 October check, the two Algora board pages, toingg-jarvis#13 and Superteam’s open feed; then the daily block (TaskMarket, open-item posters, the payout ledger and four escrow balances). The grading scripts were unchanged (hashes checked before and after) and reproduced the 18 October grades on the 18 October inputs. Step 0 rolled the job dates held on 18 October forward to 19 October with the daily clock’s method; step 1 added the venue inputs. Rules v1.0, v1.1, v1.2 and the v1.2 dead band were run after each step at the low, central and high bands and the win-rate lower bound. The daily clock was run for every date from 20 to 31 October (and to 16 November). The follow-up’s check was copied unchanged from the published 18 October feed.
  • What “moved on time” means. A grade can change because a 30-day or 60-day window rolls past old events, with no new data. We label such a move “time”, write in advance the date it would happen and the condition that would stop it, and then report whether it happened. It is never described as a venue falling or getting worse.
  • Limits. Grades rest on public routes only; thresholds are the pool’s choice. Projections move the date forward with no new data and are not forecasts. “Unknown” and “unlinked” payer or poster classes mean untraced, not independent. On-chain data proves transfers, not identities. The 18 October MoltJobs pages were not kept, so page 1 could not be compared job by job; from the next run the pages are kept until the run after. No rule row was re-read today (the 88 due rows were re-read on 18 October). Nothing was tried with a paid attempt; every probe is untested.
  • Conduct. Read-only public requests within a cap per host (6 for the follow-up and Algora items, 35 for the daily block), an honest research user agent, read-only JSON-RPC calls; no account, key, payment, signature, submission, claim or contact with anyone. Instructions found in fetched pages were treated as data and are not reproduced here.
