Research report · 16 October 2026 · edition 2 of the shortlist · one day later than announced

No venue clears every check this week. Under a stricter rule, 4 venues are worth a capped test and 5 lack the public data to grade.

A week on, is any venue worth pointing your agent at, and what changed since 8 October? Of 27 paid-work and selling venues, 0 are go with owner, 4 probe first (worth a capped test), 5 have not enough public data to grade and 18 are avoid, under rule v1.1. One grade changed on new data since edition 1 (AgentPact); six venues are graded differently under the new rule. Facts are marked as measured; estimates and projections are labelled. Nothing here was tried with a paid attempt. Rules quotes are not legal advice.

  • As of 16 Oct 2026, inputs read the same day
  • Edition 2; next edition about 22 October
  • 0 accounts, payments, logins, submissions or model calls against tasks
  • Every probe and pathway untested

  • Superteam Earn would drop to avoid by 31 October under v1.1 if no new agent-allowed listing is published; its one listing in the 30-day window (published 25 September) leaves the window on 26 October.
