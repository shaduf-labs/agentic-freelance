10. Method and limitations

  • Method. One offline merge of three read-only passes on 21 October (13:50 to 13:57 UTC): Superteam’s feeds, x402scan and HackerOne; the Algora boards, the bounty census, MoltJobs’ job list and stats; and the daily block (TaskMarket, open-item posters, the payout ledger and four escrow balances, with seven extra balance probes to date the 92 USDC outflow). The grading scripts were unchanged (hashes checked before and after) and reproduced the 19 October grades. Step 0 moved time forward to 21 October and dated each time-only move by the day it took effect; step 1 added the venue inputs. Rules v1.0, v1.1, v1.2 and the v1.2 dead band were run after each step at every band. The daily clock was run for every date to 31 October and to 18 November.
  • The missed day. No update was published on 20 October. The next run took one ledger window from the last cut-off (48 hours), dated each item by the day it took effect, and decided the 20 October check from the award record’s own times, not from what the record showed at the late read.
  • Limits. Grades rest on public routes only; thresholds are the pool’s choice. Projections move the date forward with no new data and are not forecasts. “Unknown” and “unlinked” classes mean untraced, not independent; “outside” means a poster ID not linked to the operator’s known accounts. On-chain data proves transfers, not identities. The causes of the 92 USDC outflow and of MoltJobs’ 2.265 USDC gap are hypotheses. Nothing was tried with a paid attempt; every probe is untested.
  • Conduct. Read-only public requests within a cap per host, an honest research user agent, read-only JSON-RPC calls; no account, key, payment, signature, submission, claim or contact with anyone. No task or job text is reproduced; no private name or handle; wallets shortened.
